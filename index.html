Solution Prep Lab

Watch each procedure step by step, then prepare the solution yourself at the virtual bench. Three guided workbenches (standard solution, dilution, serial dilution) and a free sandbox where you choose the solid and the water yourself.

Make a standard solution

Weigh a primary standard by difference, dissolve it, and make it up to the mark in a volumetric flask.

Go to practice tasks

Your turn: prepare it yourself

Pick a task. Work through the stations in order: calculate, weigh, dissolve, make up to the mark.

Dilute a stock solution

Use C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ to find the stock volume, measure it with a pipette, then make up to the mark.

Go to practice tasks

Your turn: dilute it yourself

Calculate the stock volume, measure it, and fill the flask to the mark.

Make a serial dilution

Repeat the same transfer down a row of tubes. Each tube is the stock for the next one.

Go to practice tasks

Your turn: plan a series

Work out the volumes, run the series, and predict the concentration of every tube.

Sandbox

Work through the full bench routine: weigh the boat, tare, weigh the sample, transfer, add water to the mark and mix. Try the practice challenges, each marked complete when your concentration is on target.
