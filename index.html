Solubility rules to use
  • Salts of Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺ and nitrates (NO₃⁻) are always soluble.
  • Chlorides, bromides and iodides are soluble, except with Ag⁺, Pb²⁺ and Hg₂²⁺.
  • Sulfates are soluble, except with Ba²⁺, Pb²⁺ and Ca²⁺ (slightly) and Sr²⁺.
  • Carbonates, phosphates, hydroxides and sulfides are insoluble, except with Group 1 cations and NH₄⁺. Ba(OH)₂ and Ca(OH)₂ are slightly soluble.
  • Carbonates react with acid to give CO₂ gas and water.
  • A strong acid and a strong base react to give water (neutralisation).
